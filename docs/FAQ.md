# Frequently Asked Questions

## General

**Q: Is my data secure?**
A: All data is stored locally on your device. No cloud sync.

**Q: What platforms are supported?**
A: Windows, macOS, Linux (desktop), and web browsers.

**Q: How do I backup my data?**
A: Export your data from Settings > Backup.

## Technical

**Q: What database is used?**
A: SQLite for local storage.

**Q: Can I self-host?**
A: Yes, using Docker or manual deployment.
